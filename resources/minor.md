Each course must be completed with a grade of C- or better, with the exception of the two introductory physics courses, to count toward the minor.

A grade of B- or better is required in two of the introductory physics courses.
Minimum Credits for Minor: 15

For further information: [Physics | Minor](https://catalog.cornell.edu/programs/physics-minor/#text)
