# International Opportunities

Research programs for physics undergraduates outside the US. Funding, eligibility, and application timelines vary, so check each program's website before you apply.


| Program | Location | Length | Paid/fee | Research type | Eligibility notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [CERN Summer Student Programme](https://home.web.cern.ch/summer-student-programme) | Geneva, Switzerland | 8 to 13 weeks | Yes (stipend) | Experimental, computing, engineering |  Open to undergraduates and master's students with at least 6 semesters of completed study | 
| [Perimeter Institute START](https://perimeterinstitute.ca/psi-start-program) | Waterloo, Canada | 2 week summer school | no fee | Theoretical physics | Check website |
| [IFJ PAN Particle Physics Programme](https://ppss.ifj.edu.pl/) | Kraków, Poland | 4 weeks | unpaid | Theoretical physics | Junior and above ; familiarity with Linux and C++ (or another modern high-level programming language). |
| [DAAD RISE Germany](https://www.daad.de/rise/en/rise-germany/) | Germany | 3 months | Yes (stipend) | Varies by host lab | undergraduate students from North American, British and Irish universities |
| [Kupcinet-Getz International Summer School](https://info.weizmann.ac.il/en/kupcinet-getz-international-summer-program/) | Rehovot, Israel | 8 weeks | Yes | Math/CS, physics, chemistry, life sciences | Undergrads who have completed at least 1 year of university. Open to all nationalities.
| [DESY Summer Student Programme](https://summerstudents.desy.de/) | Hamburg and Zeuthen, Germany | 7-8 weeks | Yes | Particle, astroparticle, photon science, accelerators | Generally seniors | 
| [PRISMA++ Internship Programme](https://prisma.uni-mainz.de/en/prisma-internship-programm/#internship%20candidates) | Mainz, Germany | 8-12 weeks | covers housing and travel | Particle, hadron, nuclear physics | Undergraduates and master's students preferably with research experience | 
| [Max Planck Matter to Life](https://mattertolife.maxplanckschools.org/uro-research-internship) | Germany | 10 weeks | Yes | Physics of living matter, bio/soft matter, interdisciplinary | Undergrduates with 2+ semesters done by the start |
| [OIST Research Internship](https://www.oist.jp/admissions/research-internship/apply-research-internship) | Okinawa, Japan | 4–6 months |2,400 JPY per working day allowance. 5,000 JPY application fee. | Physics, quantum science, chemistry, biology, math, engineering | Junior and Senior, master's students, or recent grads, any nationality. |
| [ICTS Long Term Visiting Students Program](https://www.icts.res.in/academic/long-term-visiting-student-program) | Bengaluru, India | 1–2 semesters | Yes | Physical sciences and math. Includes astrophysics, biological physics, statistical physics, and fluid dynamics. | Bachelor's or master's students entering their final year, in their final year, or recently graduated. | 
| [SOKENDAI-KEK Summer Student Program](https://www.kek.jp/en/education/sokendai/ssp) | Tsukuba and Tokai, Japan | Up to 8 weeks (theory groups up to 4) | Yes | Particle and nuclear physics, accelerator science, detectors, materials, lattice QCD and theory | Juniors and Seniors, and early master's students. | 
| [PSI Summer Student Programme](https://www.psi.ch/en/summerstudents) | Villigen, Switzerland | 8–12 weeks | Yes | Projects across PSI: neutron and muon science, photon science, particle physics, computing and theory | Seniors or masters student | 
| [MPIA Summer Internship](https://www.mpia.de) | Heidelberg, Germany | 3 months | Yes | Astronomy and astrophysics, instrumentation | Juniors and Seniors |
| [USEQIP + Undergraduate Research Award (IQC, Waterloo)](https://uwaterloo.ca/institute-for-quantum-computing/useqip) | Waterloo, Canada | 12-week |  | Quantum information, theory and experiment |Mostly juniors and seniors |
